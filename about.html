Biography

I work on web-scale search at Weixin AI, Tencent, where I lead the offline content-understanding, retrieval and pre-ranking stack behind Weixin Search — the search engine serving WeChat's user base. My interests sit at the intersection of large language models and search systems: how to represent and retrieve content across tens of billions of documents, and how to make LLMs genuinely useful inside a production ranking funnel rather than just in a demo.

I started at Tencent as an intern in SNG in 2018 and joined Tencent Cloud full-time in 2019, back when conversational AI was the thing everyone was building. I spent that period shipping chatbots — open-domain chit-chat, FAQ retrieval, and KGQA over structured knowledge. In 2020 I moved internally to WeChat Mini Program Search, working on ranking and on squeezing BERT into production latency budgets with TensorRT. When Tencent consolidated its search efforts into Weixin Search in 2021, my team came along — and we won the internal bake-off against several competing engines. That system is what powers WeChat search today.

Since then I have rebuilt the offline content-understanding and retrieval/pre-ranking architecture for both AI Search and main search from the ground up; the version running in production today is that rewrite. I have also led re-architectures of several vertical searches — image search, text-selection search and comment search — and work closely with the fine-ranking and relevance stack.
